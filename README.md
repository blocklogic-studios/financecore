# FinanceCore

Canadian multi-bank finance for Minecraft Bedrock 1.26+.

Real institutions, physical **CAD tender**, card embossing, ATMs, tellers, POS, e-Transfer (CarrierCore phone-aware), and direct deposit.

## Features

- Banks & credit unions: RBC, TD, Scotiabank, BMO, CIBC, National Bank, Desjardins, Vancity, Servus, Coast Capital, Tangerine, EQ Bank
- Accounts: chequing, savings, business, credit
- **CAD bills & coins** ($5–$100, nickel–toonie)
- ATM withdraw → physical tender; deposit ← tender from inventory
- Card embosser (debit / credit / business + PIN)
- e-Transfer by phone (resolves CarrierCore SIMs when present)
- Direct deposit (payroll-style)
- POS merchant charge
- Cash drawer & vault
- Optional cost-per-block

## Install

1. Download the `.mcaddon` from [Releases](../../releases) (or build from source)
2. Enable Behavior + Resource packs
3. Turn on **Beta APIs**
